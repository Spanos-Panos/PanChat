# PanChat privacy policy (MVP)

**Last updated:** May 2026

This document mirrors the in-app policy at **Account → Privacy policy** or the web `/privacy` view.

## What we collect

- Email, username, display name, optional birth date
- Profile photo (optional upload)
- Chat messages and attached tasks
- Session cookie and basic server logs

## Why

- Authentication and chat functionality
- Abuse prevention (rate limits)

## Cookies

One **necessary** session cookie (`token`, httpOnly). No analytics cookies in the current MVP.

## Your rights (MVP features)

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Export data | Account → **Download my data** |
| Delete account | Account → **Delete account** (confirm email) |

## Contact

Portfolio / learning project — contact the repository owner for data questions.
